## 0.1.0

- First version: a dev console that drops down from the top of the screen (the ` key or the bubble icon), with soft teal pixel text and bubbles.
- Commands to teleport (`tp`), sit (`sit`, `stand`), emote, chat (`say`) and look things up (`players`, `session`, `net`, `mods`, `fps`, `where`, `version`).
- Toys: `fortune`, `roll`, `flip`, `8ball`, `duck`, `sudo`, `hack` and `train`, plus `bighead` and `tiny`, which only you see.
- Your own commands with `alias`, keys that run commands with `bind`, and `autoexec.cfg` for commands to run every time you enter the world (`reload` reads them again after editing).
- `motd` shows the welcome message again with a new tip, plus your own message from `motd.txt`.
- `open` does what clicking any mod's icon does (and `open <mod> right` its right-click). Outfit Slots, Teleport Spots, Buddy Up, Emote Sync, Desk Swarm, Session Manager, Chat Range, Desk Snap, Photo Mode and Song Player add their own commands in their newest versions (`outfit`, `spot`, `buddy`, `sync`, `swarm`, `kick`, `range`, `snap`, `photo`, `song`...).
- Not tried in the game yet.
