## 0.1.0

- First version: a tiny games console in a window (the handheld icon), with a 192 x 128, 16-colour screen inside a pastel handheld in four colours. Its buttons light up as you press them, and you can click them too.
- Comes with Bubble Pop, a little game about popping the bubbles a kid blows on an island.
- Make your own carts in Lua with the code editor: coloured code, undo, copy and paste, a sheet of every function, and errors that take you to their line.
- Your carts save by themselves in Documents/On-Together/Soap Box.
- The window, Bubble Pop and the code editor work in the game. The copy saved when you change Bubble Pop, renaming carts and jumping to an error's line haven't all been tried yet.
