## 0.3.0

- An icon in the icon row at the bottom-right of the screen: two cheering friends, with a green dot while sync is on. Click it to turn sync on or off, and right-click it to show or hide the group window.
- 0.2.0, 0.2.1 and 0.3.0 work together, so not everyone has to update at once.

## 0.2.1

- Messages, the "Joining..." prompt and the group window show players' in-game names the way their name tags do, colours included. (They used to show Steam names.)

## 0.2.0

- If you're busy when someone picks a focus activity (typing, a window open, mid-emote, in the air), you join as soon as you're free, for up to 30 seconds.
- A **Join now** button leaves the chat box, closes the window or stops the emote, and joins straight away.
- Standing up tells the group, so nobody joins an activity that's already over.
- With Focus Anywhere installed, you can join on any ground.

## 0.1.0

- First version: emotes start together for the group, and you join each other's focus activities.
