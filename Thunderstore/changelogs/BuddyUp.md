## 0.2.1

- With Bubble Shell (the new dev console mod): `buddy` shows your buddies, `buddy <name>` asks someone (or says yes), `buddy no <name>` / `buddy bye <name>`, `ride <buddy>`, `walk <buddy>`, `buddy stop` and `buddy shake`. Not tried in the game yet.

## 0.2.0

- One leader per group: if your buddy climbs onto your head or starts walking with you while you're riding or walking with them, you stop yours (the newest one wins). Walking with someone who walks with someone else takes you to the one at the front.
- Ride and Walk switch straight over: Walk while you're on a head hops you off and walks, and Ride while you're walking climbs on (it waits a moment if you're on the stairs or mid-step).
- Walking together keeps pace with your buddy instead of trailing behind, without the stop-and-go.
- Get off, Shake off and losing your buddy put you back on your feet if you were standing when you climbed on.
- Sitting together: when your buddy stands up, so do you, unless you've picked your own activity. Emote Sync's focus join counts as sitting down together, and you won't sit down beside a desk that's flying with Desk Swarm.

## 0.1.0

- First version: buddy up with a friend, ride on their head (riders can ride riders), walk together beside or behind them, and sit down beside them in the same activity.
