## 0.1.4

- With Bubble Shell (the new dev console mod): `outfit` lists your outfits, `outfit 4` (or an outfit's name) puts one on, `outfit next` the next one, and `outfit copy 2 5` copies the outfit in slot 2 into slot 5. Not tried in the game yet.

## 0.1.3

- The icon row has a tray: click the gear button at its right end to open it. Icons you tuck away wait there and work just the same, and a green dot on the gear means a tucked-away mod is switched on.
- Right-click the gear (or click **Arrange icons** in the tray) to arrange the row: drag icons to reorder them, drag them into the tray and back, or click one to tuck it away or put it back. **Reset** puts every icon back. The arrangement is saved in `BepInEx/config/IconRow.json` and shared by every mod in this set. Update the other mods too, so all their icons can be arranged.

## 0.1.2

- In the Normal window mode (key 1), the icon and the quick-swap window also hide and show with the game's eye button in the top-right corner, as they already did in split screen and Desk mode.

## 0.1.1

- In the game's split-screen and Desk window modes (keys 2-4), the icon and the quick-swap window hide and show with the game's own UI when you click its eye button. Before, they always hid in Desk mode and stayed on in split screen. In the Normal window mode they always show.
