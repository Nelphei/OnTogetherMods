## 0.4.3

- In the Normal window mode (key 1), Photo Mode's icon and messages also hide and show with the game's eye button in the top-right corner.
- If the game's UI is hidden that way when you start Photo Mode, the controls panel and messages stay hidden too. Photos still work, and **F9** or **Esc** still leaves.

## 0.4.2

- In the game's split-screen and Desk window modes (keys 2-4), Photo Mode's icon and messages hide and show with the game's own UI when you click its eye button. Before, they always hid in Desk mode and stayed on in split screen. In the Normal window mode they always show.

## 0.4.1

- The icon row hides while Photo Mode is on, so it stays out of your way. Leave with **F9** or **Esc**, and **F1** shows or hides the controls panel.
- Photo Mode's messages hide in the game's Desk window mode (key 4, the small see-through window), like the other mods' windows.

## 0.4.0

- A camera icon in the icon row at the bottom-right of the screen. Click it to start or leave Photo Mode, and right-click it for the controls panel. The row is never in your photos.

## 0.3.0

- Type in chat while Photo Mode is on: show the chat box with **T**, then press **Enter** or click it. The photo keys pause while you type, and **Esc** stops typing instead of leaving Photo Mode.

## 0.2.2

- Other mods' messages and windows stay on screen while Photo Mode hides the game's UI. They still stay out of your photos.

## 0.2.1

- The controls panel stacks neatly with other mods' windows.

## 0.2.0 and earlier

- Free camera, hidden UI and PNG photos. **T** shows or hides the chat box.
