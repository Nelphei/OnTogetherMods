## 0.2.0

- Every card has its own picture now. The 22 major cards are little cat scenes: a calico kitten stepping off a cliff for The Fool, a tuxedo cat with the scales for Justice, a reaper kitty on a white pony for Death, and so on.
- The aces show a cat's paw reaching out of a cloud with the suit's thing: a heart-tipped wand, a teacup, a sword or a star coin.
- The court cards are cats too: Pages are kittens in feathered caps, Knights ride a white pony, and Queens and Kings sit crowned on their thrones, each suit in its own country.
- The number cards show their suit's things in a pattern, with a different kitten peeking up at them on every card. They become little scenes in a later update.
- Not tried in the game yet.

## 0.1.0

- First version: tarot readings in a window (the moon cat icon), with a deck of 78 pastel cat cards, each suit in its own colour.
- Shuffle the deck (a riffle, as often as you like), wash it (the cards swirl round and some turn upside down) and cut it into three piles, then deal three cards or pick them yourself from a fan.
- Click a card to turn it over, and again to see it big. Reversed cards lie upside down; Options can turn reversals off, or read with the major cards only.
- Your deck and your reading are saved between games.
- Pictures so far: Strength and The Star. The other cards show a paw print until theirs are drawn.
- Not tried in the game yet.
