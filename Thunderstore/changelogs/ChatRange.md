## 0.2.2

- In the game's split-screen and Desk window modes (keys 2-4), the icon and the list hide and show with the game's own UI when you click its eye button. Before, they always hid in Desk mode and stayed on in split screen. In the Normal window mode they always show.

## 0.2.1

- The icon and the list hide in the game's Desk window mode (key 4, the small see-through window), and come back when you switch to another window mode.
- The icon hides while Photo Mode is on.

## 0.2.0

- A speech bubble icon in the icon row at the bottom-right of the screen switches the list on and off. Your choice is remembered between games.
