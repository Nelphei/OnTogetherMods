## 0.2.0

- Five new flying shapes: Heart (a heart standing up over your head), Wheel (a Ferris wheel turning over your head), Twister (a spinning spiral winding up around you), Wave (a row beside you with a ripple running along it) and Stairs (steps climbing into the sky ahead of you).
- Landing shapes: Row, Class, Circle, Table and Grid. The desks come down and sit on the ground: side by side with you, in rows facing you, in a circle with you, across a table from each other, or in an even square grid like drones before a show. They hop up while you move off and land again when you stop.
- Everyone in a swarm needs 0.2.0 for the new shapes. A follower on 0.1.0 stays in the last shape it knew.
