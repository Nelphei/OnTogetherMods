## 0.2.0

- Colour the colon after your name in chat ("Name: hello"). On the Name tab, click the **:** tile after your letters and pick a colour. Your message keeps the game's normal text colour, and other players see the coloured colon without the mod.
- The game's "has joined the server!" and "joined your pomodoro session." messages, and the "(Host)" label in the player list, come right after your name, so they take the colon colour too.

## 0.1.1

- In a world, the icon and the window hide in the game's Desk window mode (key 4, the small see-through window), and come back when you switch to another window mode. If you were typing, your keys go back to the game until the window shows again.
- The icon hides while Photo Mode is on.
